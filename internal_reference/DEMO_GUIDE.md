# Demonstration Guide — Denpasar Customer Demo v3.0

Use `CUSTOMER_DEMO_SCRIPT.md` as the speaker script.

## Before the meeting

1. Run `RESET_DEMO_WINDOWS.bat`.
2. Run `START_GEODISCOVER_WINDOWS.bat`.
3. Sign in and confirm the Executive Dashboard shows 400 Grab, 562 Google, 53 matched pairs, 39 Tier 1, 139 Tier 2, and zero strict release records.
4. Open Map Intelligence once to allow the OpenStreetMap tiles to load.
5. Keep the polished workbook open as a backup: `sample_output/GeoDiscover_Denpasar_Customer_Demo.xlsx`.

## Customer flow

Dashboard → Grab vs Google → Tier 1 candidate → Google Exclusion → Entity Resolution → Map → Evidence → QA → Deliverables.

## Do not change during the demo

- Do not tick unsupported Google checks.
- Do not mark any record `GOOGLE_ABSENT_VERIFIED`.
- Do not approve any demonstration record for customer release.
- Do not claim citywide or national coverage.
