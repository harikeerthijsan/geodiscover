# Product Requirements — Customer Capability POC v2.0

The build targets the first customer-proof slice of JSAN GeoDiscover:

1. Multi-source adapter management with live/mock/file modes
2. Grab-versus-Google comparison
3. Explainable entity resolution and reversible deduplication
4. Complete Google-absence verification
5. Evidence repository and timeline
6. Calling and local-research workflows
7. Independent QA and release governance
8. Coordinate-based GIS map
9. Customer deliverable and audit export
10. PostgreSQL/PostGIS, migrations, worker, and deployment scaffolding

National-scale crawling, a commercial telephony integration, S3 object storage, and a verified real sample remain governed pilot/production work rather than fabricated POC content.
