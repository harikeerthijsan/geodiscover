JSAN GEODISCOVER™ — EXECUTIVE CUSTOMER DEMO v5.0
=================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This package tells one simple customer story:

1. Compare authorised HERE, Google and Grab POI extracts.
2. Show confirmed matches and evidence-backed candidate tiers.
3. Prioritise a Strong 10/25 validation sample.
4. Explain real coordinates, evidence and cost controls.
5. Block customer release until enrichment and independent QA are complete.

FIRST-TIME WINDOWS SETUP
------------------------
1. Extract the ZIP to a simple folder, for example:
   C:\JSAN\GeoDiscover_Executive_Demo_v5

2. Double-click:
   START_GEODISCOVER_WINDOWS.bat

3. On the first run, Notepad opens backend\.env.
   Set a long JWT_SECRET and a new DEMO_ADMIN_PASSWORD.
   Save and close Notepad.

4. Run START_GEODISCOVER_WINDOWS.bat again.

5. The browser opens:
   http://localhost:8000

6. Sign in with DEMO_ADMIN_EMAIL and DEMO_ADMIN_PASSWORD from backend\.env.

THREE-MAP AREA COMPARISON
-------------------------
Open http://localhost:8000/engine/ and choose "Area Comparison" to draw a
radius on the HERE map and compare HERE, Google and Grab across every POI
category inside it.
